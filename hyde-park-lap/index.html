Numbering keeps the 18 curated corners. On the faster line a few of them (T2, T4, T8 and T13) are now taken flat. Apex is the gap between the car's inside wheels and the inside track edge at the apex; Clipped means wheels on the kerb. vs old is the change in minimum speed from the previous line. Tap a corner to jump to it.

Racing line. The line is solved for minimum lap time: an optimal-control problem over the whole lap, between the real left and right track edges every 2 m. The car's edge may reach the track edge (wheels on the kerb) but never go beyond it, in case there are walls. It trades corner speed against distance and straight-line exits, so it clips apexes and uses the full width where the road widens. Speeds are then recomputed on that line with the same car model as before.

Old line. The previous line was the minimum-curvature path with a 0.25 m margin: . Switch it on with Old line on the map (dashed).

Car. A point-mass F1-style car: 800 kg with driver, 760 kW, grip coefficient 1.55, downforce and drag from a high-downforce street set-up, braking capped at 5.4 g. Speeds are steady-state, with no gears, tyre wear, kerb riding or traffic.

Gradient. Heights come from the Environment Agency LIDAR, with the Serpentine Bridge and the Piccadilly Underpass decked over. Climbs slow the car and drops help it, in both acceleration and braking.
